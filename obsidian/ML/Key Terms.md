UCR : The percentage of claims in a [**response**](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/glossary#response) that aren't [**grounded**](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/glossary#grounding). For example, if an LLM's response makes 10 claims but only 1 is grounded, the UCR is 90%.

A high UCR implies that an LLM is [**hallucinating**](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/glossary#hallucination) too frequently.

See also [**citation precision**](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/glossary#citation-precision) and [**citation recall**](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/glossary#citation-recall).
